Diagnosis · Root Cause Analysis
Key Gaps Identified
Six critical performance gaps driving Q1 underperformance
📉
Pipeline Conversion Failure
Volume decline attributable to insufficient follow-through on qualified pipeline opportunities — lack of structured conversion cadence.
📦
Untapped Product Lines
Ocean Freight, Courier, Warehousing, Air Imports and General Cargo Exports are available but not actively sold or targeted.
⚠️
Perishables Over-Dependence
Revenue and GP remain heavily concentrated in perishable air exports — a single-point-of-failure revenue model with inherent volatility.
💸
Pricing Discipline Erosion
Margin compression from inconsistent quoting practices. GP target of 15% is achievable but requires enforcement across all accounts.
🔴
Customer Claims Impact
Unresolved customer claims, particularly in the perishable segment, are eroding profitability and increasing operational overhead.
📊
Revenue Mix Imbalance
Composition is disproportionately weighted toward low-margin product categories, reducing blended GP contribution.

Commercial Excellence · Pillars 1–4
Product & Customer Mastery
Foundational disciplines for winning and retaining high-value customers
📚
Product Knowledge
Master every service in our portfolio — quality standards, transit times, regulatory requirements, and handling specifications. The team that knows its product best wins the deal.
🎯
Customer Understanding
Research each prospect's supply chain needs, import volumes, and pain points. Tailor every proposal to their specific requirements — generic pitches lose to targeted ones.
💹
Pricing Strategy
Price competitively and profitably. Factor in all costs — handling, transport, compliance — and maintain the 15% GP floor without exception. Discounting must be strategic, not reflexive.
🏗️
Infrastructure Capability
Lead with our physical assets: temperature-controlled storage, pharma handling, refrigerated transport, and specialized equipment. Demonstrate capability before the customer asks for it.
11
Commercial Excellence · Pillars 5–8
Service, Partnerships & Growth
Execution disciplines that create competitive differentiation and long-term retention
⭐
Service Excellence
Respond promptly, update proactively, and resolve issues without hesitation. Customers who experience outstanding service become long-term advocates — not just accounts.
🤝
Strategic Partnerships
Build structured relationships with local producers, exporters, and freight agents. Partnerships generate consistent referral flow and build market credibility across segments.
📱
Digital Marketing
Leverage LinkedIn, web presence, and targeted digital channels. Share success stories, case studies, and capability content. A visible brand attracts inbound enquiries at zero incremental cost.
🌍
Industry Networking
Attend relevant trade shows and freight events. Face-to-face engagement with decision-makers creates opportunities no cold call can replicate — prioritize presence at the right events.
